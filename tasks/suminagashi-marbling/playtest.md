# Suminagashi — Ink Dissolution

An interactive Japanese paper-marbling (suminagashi) canvas rendered with WebGL.
Drag across the paper to float ink onto the surface and watch it dissolve into
slow, flowing marbled patterns over a few seconds.

## Interactions

- Drag (press and move the mouse across the paper): trails ink and creates the
  marbling. Longer, curving drags produce richer patterns.
- Colour swatches (bottom-centre row): click a dot to change the ink colour —
  multi (巡), sumi black (墨), indigo (藍), vermilion (朱), pine green (松葉).
- 自動演出 (auto): toggle an automatic ink performance that paints on its own.
- 洗い流す (wash): clear the paper back to a clean sheet.

## What to explore

- Make several separate drag strokes and watch each plume of ink diffuse and
  settle over roughly two to four seconds before acting again.
- Change the ink colour between strokes and layer different colours.
- Toggle 自動演出 to watch the automatic performance, then use 洗い流す to reset.
